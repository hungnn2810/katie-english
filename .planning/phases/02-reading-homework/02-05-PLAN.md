    **Step 1 — Teacher creates a reading homework (READ-01, READ-02, READ-03):**
    1. Log in as the teacher.
    2. Navigate to /teacher/homework. Confirm four filter tabs visible: ALL, 🔤 Phonics, 🎤 Speaking, 📖 Reading. Confirm two CTA buttons visible: "New Reading" (green-secondary gradient) and "New Homework" (default).

    4. Enter name "UAT Reading 1".
    5. Click "+ Add Matching Activity". A card appears with "📷 Matching" badge, "Activity 1" label, ≡ drag handle, "Remove" button, and an upload trigger reading "Click to upload images / Each image becomes a matching pair".
    6. Click the upload trigger. Select 3 image files from your local disk. Confirm: 3 pair cards in a grid, each showing the image thumbnail and the filename (without extension) pre-filled in the word label input. **READ-01 ✓**
    7. Edit one word label to confirm the input is interactive. Confirm "+ Add images" button visible.
    8. Click "+ Add Fill-in-blank Activity". Second card appears with "✏️ Fill in the Blank" badge, "Activity 2" label.
    9. Click "+ Add sentence". Sentence card appears with textarea, two choice rows (one marked correct by default), "+ Add choice" button, "Remove sentence" button.
    10. Type sentence: "The cat sat on the ___". Type word choice 1: "mat" (default correct). Type word choice 2: "log". Click "+ Add choice", type "bat". Click the radio next to "log" — confirm correct mark moves to "log" and "mat" un-marks. Click back to "mat". **READ-02 ✓**
    11. Drag the FILL_BLANK activity card (via ≡ handle) ABOVE the MATCH card. Confirm order changes visually. **READ-03 ✓**
    12. Click "Save Homework". Confirm redirect to /teacher/homework with the new "UAT Reading 1" card visible showing "2 activities" preview.
    13. Confirm via API or list re-visit that order is preserved (FILL_BLANK first, MATCH second).

    **Step 2 — Teacher assigns the homework to a class:**
    1. From /teacher/homework, on the "UAT Reading 1" card, click Assign.
    2. Select the test student's class, pick a due date in the future, save.

    **Step 3 — Student plays the homework (READ-04, READ-05, READ-06):**
    1. Log out, log in as the student.
    2. Navigate to /game/homework. Confirm the new reading assignment is in the list with "📖 2 activities" chip.
    3. Click the assignment. Confirm route /game/reading/{sessionId} loads with gameBg gradient, 2 progress pills (current = accent yellow, upcoming = white/20), "Activity 1 of 2" counter.
    4. First activity is the FILL_BLANK. Confirm sentence "The cat sat on the ___" with blank box visible and 3 choice buttons.
    5. Click the WRONG choice. Confirm shake (400ms) then auto-advance. **READ-05 (one-shot, no retry) ✓**
    6. Activity 2 is MATCH. Confirm 3 image cards in a row, 3 word buttons (shuffled order).
    7. Click an image — primary-blue 4px border appears.
    8. Click a WRONG word — both shake then deselect.
    9. Re-select the image.
    10. Click the CORRECT word — both turn green with ✓ overlay on the image. Both locked. **READ-04 ✓**
    11. Continue matching the remaining 2 pairs.
    12. After last pair locks, confirm ~500ms celebration → "Saving your score…" → results screen.
    13. Results screen shows 🎉, "Homework Complete!", score number colored by scoreHexColor, two activity summary cards ("Fill in the Blank: 0/1 sentences correct", "Matching: 3/3 pairs matched"), Finish button. **READ-06 ✓**
    14. Click Finish. Confirm route back to /game/homework. Assignment now shows "Best: {score}%" chip.

    **Step 4 — Confirm score persisted in DB:**
    1. Either: log in as teacher and inspect via API (`GET /game/session/{id}` returns the score), or
    2. Re-open the student app, click the same assignment — observe "Best" chip remains.

    5. Add one FILL_BLANK activity with a sentence missing `___` — confirm inline error "Each sentence must contain ___ for the blank." and Save blocks.
